# Hoi, I'm Joep 👋

**Maker from Eindhoven. Creative soul, a head full of ideas, and a pair of hands that knows what to do with them.**

By day I run [Klussir](https://klussir.nl), my one-man handyman and painting business: carpentry, repairs, paint, light installations. By night I build the ideas that won't leave me alone. The approach is the same for a wardrobe under a sloped ceiling as for a game engine: measure twice, understand why it works, and leave it nicer than I found it.

I'm not a trained programmer. I'm a builder who discovered that software is just another material, and that with AI as a co-developer I can finally make the things I've been sketching for years.

## 🛠️ On the workbench

### 🏙️ Raatstad
A 3D city builder made of stackable hexagon blocks. Every module connects seamlessly to its neighbours, everything is at true-to-life scale, and you can walk through whatever you build. It runs in the browser, a Unity version is in the works, and a beta is on its way to [itch.io](https://joepdoet.itch.io).

### 💡 [L.I.F.E.](https://github.com/joepk1989/LIFE)
*Luminous Interactive Frame Experience.* A 150 × 150 cm illuminated frame with programmable LEDs that you step into and pose inside. A photo frame like no other. Designed in SketchUp, powered by an ESP32 running WLED and a Raspberry Pi, and built with my own hands.

### 🕰️ [Timeline](https://github.com/joepk1989/timeline)
A timeline creator for anything.

### 🐄 Happy Animals
A browser game full of hopping farm animals that make a lot of noise. Because not everything has to be serious.

### 💥 [2D-destruction](https://github.com/joepk1989/2D-destruction)
A small game experiment about breaking things, made with AI.

## 🧭 How I work

- I think in 3D and sketch before I build.
- I want to know *why* something works, not just *that* it works.
- I like systems where small parts click together into something bigger. Hexagons are a good example.
- Away from the workbench: flying a drone, modding games, and music.
